    Terms

    November Partner Programme terms

    These terms are between you and Geeslane Technologies. They apply from the moment you send the registration form. Sending the form means you agree to them.

    What this is

    This is a 30-day referral arrangement for November 2026. You introduce new clients to Geeslane. You are not an employee, not an agent, and not a partner in the legal sense. You cannot set Geeslane’s prices, collect client money, sign for Geeslane, or say that you work for Geeslane.

    You register as a person. A business does not register as a partner. A referred business sends its own service request through your invite link.

    When it runs

    The programme uses West Africa Time (Africa/Lagos). Your invite link attributes a request only from 1 November 2026 through 30 November 2026. After 30 November the link stops working for new requests. Your page stays open so you can see what you earned and which transfers Geeslane has made.

    You may register before 1 November. The link still starts counting on 1 November. Registration itself closes at the end of 30 November 2026.

    Your link

    Geeslane issues one invite link to the email on your form as soon as you register. The request form fills your code from that link. A code typed in by hand does not count. If two partners send the same person, the first request Geeslane stores for that email address keeps the referral.

    You may share the link and the flyer kit Geeslane gives you. Do not change the logo, invent a discount, or promise a result Geeslane has not agreed. Do not present the flyers as your own brand.

    What counts

    A referral counts only when all of these are true:

    • The person is new. If Geeslane was already speaking with them, the referral does not count. Geeslane’s record of that conversation is final.
    • They send the service request through your invite link in November 2026.
    • They accept Geeslane’s quote and the first payment clears between 1 and 30 November 2026. Accepting the quote includes paying the initial 50%. A one-off fee that clears in that window is the project payment.

    You earn 20% of the Geeslane fee in each counted payment, after any discount Geeslane has agreed. Domain, hosting, email, licences, and any other third-party amount are not part of that fee. Extra work quoted after the accepted offer does not earn.

    The first payment earns 20% when it clears. The second payment earns 20% when it clears, including a balance paid after 30 November. A one-off fee is one project payment. The most you can earn on one project is ₦100,000. After that cap, further payments on the same project earn nothing.

    How you are paid

    Geeslane transfers your amount after each counted client payment has cleared, to the bank account on your form. The account name must match the full name you register with. If the account details are wrong, Geeslane is not required to pay a second time. Your page shows the project name, the business name, the payment type, the date the client paid, your amount, and the receipt note for Geeslane’s transfer. It does not show the client’s phone number or email. Keep what you see on that page private.

    WhatsApp group

    You confirm that you have joined the Geeslane Refer & Earn WhatsApp group before you send the form. That group is for programme updates, marketing kits, and tips. Your welcome email repeats the group link.

    Records and law

    Geeslane’s record of the invite link, the request, the quote, and the cleared payments decides what counts. Nigerian law applies. Write first to contact@geeslane.com if something looks wrong. These terms cover this November programme only.
