Commission

How much can you earn?

You earn 20% of Geeslane’s eligible service fee on each of the two project payments.

Every project is paid in two instalments:

  • Initial payment: 50% of the agreed project fee.
  • Final payment: the remaining 50%.

You earn commission as each payment clears.

Example: eligible Geeslane service fee of NGN 300,000
PaymentClient paysYour commission
Initial payment, 50%NGN 150,000NGN 30,000
Final payment, 50%NGN 150,000NGN 30,000
TotalNGN 300,000NGN 60,000

Maximum commission per project: NGN 100,000.

Third-party costs such as domains, hosting, business email, licences and other external services are excluded from the commission calculation.

The first 50% payment must clear between 1 and 30 November 2026 for the referral to qualify.

Once a project qualifies, commission on the final 50% payment remains payable even if the client pays after November.

If the client cancels the project, the partner retains commission already paid but forfeits outstanding or future commission from that project.

FAQ

Questions partners ask

Who can join?

Any individual, anywhere. Commission is paid only to a Nigerian bank account in your own name. You confirm that you have joined the WhatsApp group before you register.

Do I need sales or technical experience?

No. You introduce potential clients. Geeslane handles the conversation, the quote, and the project.

How do I get my referral link?

It is created when you register and sent to your email, together with the link to your private partner page.

How does Geeslane contact referred clients?

They submit a request through your link. Geeslane contacts them, discusses the work, and prepares the quote.

When do I earn commission?

When a new client’s initial 50% payment clears between 1 and 30 November 2026, and again when the final 50% clears. Geeslane transfers each amount within 1–3 working days.

What happens if the client pays the balance after November?

You still earn 20% of that eligible balance, if the initial 50% cleared in November.

Are domain, hosting, and email fees included?

No. Domain registration, hosting, business email, licences, and other third-party costs are excluded.

Can existing Geeslane clients qualify?

No. Existing clients, returning clients, and businesses Geeslane was already speaking with do not qualify, even for a different service. Following Geeslane on social media does not by itself make someone an existing client.

Can I refer more than one business?

Yes. Share your link with as many new businesses as you like. Each project is capped at NGN 100,000 in commission.

How do I receive my commission?

Geeslane transfers it to the Nigerian bank account on your registration, within 1–3 working days after the client payment clears.

What happens if a client cancels?

You keep commission already transferred. Outstanding or future commission on that project is not paid. Geeslane does not refund client fees, and commission is not reversed.

Can I access my dashboard after November?

Yes. Your partner page stays open so you can follow balance payments and transfers for projects that qualified in November.

Terms

November Partner Programme terms

These terms are between you and Geeslane Technologies. They apply from the moment you send the registration form. Sending the form means you agree to them.

Programme period

Dates use West Africa Time (Africa/Lagos).

  • The programme runs from 1 November 2026 to 30 November 2026.
  • You may register before November. Registration stays open through the end of 30 November 2026.
  • Your invite link attributes a new request only during that window.
  • The client’s initial 50% payment must clear during November for the referral to qualify.
  • The final 50% payment may clear after November and still qualify for commission.

Registration

  • The programme is open to individuals worldwide. A business does not register as a partner.
  • Commission is paid only to a Nigerian bank account in your own name. The account name must match the full name on this form.
  • Registration is automatic. No manual approval is required.
  • Your personal invite link is created and emailed when registration succeeds.

Referral eligibility

  • Only new clients qualify. Existing clients and returning clients do not qualify, even if they request a different service.
  • A person or business Geeslane was already speaking with before the referral does not qualify. Geeslane’s record of that conversation is final.
  • The first request Geeslane stores for an email address keeps the referral.
  • A referral code typed in by hand does not count. The request form fills the code from your invite link.
  • A referred business sends its own service request through that link.

Commission

  • You earn 20% of the eligible Geeslane amount in each instalment. Across both instalments, that is 20% of the eligible Geeslane service fee.
  • Domain, hosting, business email, licences, and other third-party amounts are excluded.
  • Each project is paid in two instalments: 50% initial and 50% final. Commission is earned as each qualifying payment clears.
  • The most you can earn on one project is NGN 100,000.
  • Geeslane transfers the commission within 1–3 working days after each qualifying client payment clears, to the Nigerian account on your form.
  • The first 50% must clear in November. The final 50% can clear after November and still earn commission.
  • If the client cancels the project, you keep commission already paid and forfeit outstanding or future commission from that project.
  • Extra work quoted after the accepted offer does not earn.

What you must not do

  • Claim to be a Geeslane employee or authorised representative.
  • Promise a discount, delivery date, or result Geeslane has not agreed.
  • Change a quotation or negotiate on Geeslane’s behalf.
  • Collect money from a client for Geeslane.
  • Misrepresent Geeslane’s services.
  • Share confidential client information.

Your link and materials

You may share your invite link and the flyer kit Geeslane gives you. Do not change the logo or present the flyers as your own brand.

WhatsApp group
