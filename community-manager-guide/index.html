About the November Refer & Earn Programme

The November campaign gives registered partners an opportunity to earn 20% commission on successful referrals to Geeslane.

Partners share their personal referral links with potential clients. Geeslane handles the business discussions and service delivery, while partners earn commissions on eligible successful referrals.

Programme terms: https://geeslane.com/partners

2. Your Role as Community Manager

Your responsibility is to keep partners informed, encouraged and updated throughout the campaign.

Your responsibilities include:

  • Welcoming new partners into the WhatsApp group.
  • Sharing useful information about Geeslane and its services.
  • Sharing referral tips and occasional reminders.
  • Responding to general questions.
  • Sharing approved programme updates.
  • Celebrating successful referrals and payments communicated by Geeslane Admin.
  • Helping partners understand how to access and use their referral links.
  • Escalating issues that require management's attention.

Understanding the nature of this group

This is a business referral group, not a social or friendship group.

Partners do not have to participate in conversations. Some may simply read the updates and share their referral links with potential clients. That's perfectly fine.

Our goal is to help them refer potential clients, not pressure them to chat.

Reminding Partners to Follow Up With Their Prospects

As November 30 approaches, remind partners in the existing WhatsApp group to reconnect with potential clients they have already referred. This matters most for people who have received a quotation or said they are interested in starting a project.

The aim is to help partners avoid missing commission because a referred client did not complete the initial payment before the campaign deadline.

  • The initial 50% payment must clear by November 30, 2026, for the referral to qualify.
  • The remaining 50% balance can be paid after November.
  • Partners should encourage interested prospects to contact Geeslane if they need clarification about a quotation or project.
  • Partners are responsible for following up with their own prospects.
  • You only share reminders and updates in the existing WhatsApp group.

This group is a business referral channel. It is not a social or friendship group.

8. Sharing Payment Confirmations

Sharing approved payment confirmations helps demonstrate transparency and builds trust.

However, recipients' privacy must always be protected.

Before sharing a payment screenshot

StepWhat to Do
1Obtain the recipient's permission first.
2Open the screenshot in WhatsApp's image editor.
3Use the pen icon to draw a solid, opaque line over the recipient's full name.
4Completely cover the recipient's account number.
5Cover transaction references, session IDs, phone numbers and any other sensitive information.
6Check the image carefully before posting to ensure no private information is visible.
7Tag the recipient only if they have given permission.

Important:

  • Use a solid line, not a transparent highlighter.
  • Do not alter the payment amount or transaction status.
  • Never share screenshots without the recipient's consent.
  • If the recipient does not want their payment shared, celebrate with a simple text message instead.

10. Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some questions partners may ask and how you should respond.

Q: What is Geeslane?

Geeslane is a technology company helping businesses grow through website development, digital solutions and AI automation.

Q: How do I join the programme?

Visit https://geeslane.com/partners, read the programme terms and complete the registration form. After submitting the form, you can join the WhatsApp group using the link provided.

Q: How does the referral programme work?

It's simple!

  1. Register at geeslane.com/partners.
  2. Join the WhatsApp group through the link provided.
  3. Check your email for your personal Geeslane Partner Link.
  4. Share your link with potential clients.
  5. Track your referral history from your partner page.

Q: Where do I find my invite link?

Check your email inbox for a message titled "Your Geeslane Partner Link".

Your personal referral link is included in that email. Copy and share it with potential clients.

If you cannot find it, check your spam and promotions folders.

Q: Where can I view my referral history?

You can view your referral history on your personal partner page using the partner page link provided in your email.

Q: What if I can't find my partner email?

First, check your inbox, spam and promotions folders for an email titled "Your Geeslane Partner Link".

If you still cannot find it, contact Geeslane Admin for assistance.

Q: How much commission do I earn?

The November campaign offers 20% commission on successful referrals, subject to the programme terms at https://geeslane.com/partners.

Q: Can I refer more than one person?

Absolutely! You can share your personal referral link with multiple potential clients.

Q: Do I need to understand website development or AI?

Not at all! Your role is to identify potential clients and share your referral link. The Geeslane team handles the technical discussions.

Q: What if the person I refer doesn't need a website?

Geeslane offers several services, including website revamps, domain and hosting services, business consultation and AI automation. They may need something else we offer.

Q: What happens after I share my referral link?

Your referral activity can be viewed on your partner page. Geeslane will handle follow-up discussions with potential clients and validate successful conversions according to the programme terms.

Q: When will I receive my commission?

Commission eligibility and payment arrangements are subject to the programme terms. For specific payment questions, please contact Geeslane Admin.

Q: My referral hasn't appeared on my partner page. What should I do?

Contact Geeslane Admin with your referral details so the team can investigate.

Q: Can I refer businesses outside Nigeria?

You can introduce potential clients outside Nigeria, but please confirm specific service availability with management before making commitments.

Q: Can I share Geeslane's promotional materials on my social media?

Yes! You can share approved Geeslane promotional materials and your personal referral link with your network.

Q: What if a potential client wants to speak directly with Geeslane?

That's perfectly fine. They can use the contact information on Geeslane's website or follow the relevant referral process.

Q: Who do I contact if I have a problem?

For general questions, contact the Community Manager.

For missing links, referral validation, commissions, payments or programme-related issues, contact Geeslane Admin on 07044902301.

Golden rule: If you don't know the answer, don't guess. Confirm with management.

11. Our November Goal 💚

Our goal is simple:

Help partners connect Geeslane to potential clients and reward them for successful referrals.

As Community Manager, your success is not measured by how many messages you post or how active the WhatsApp group looks.

It is about ensuring that:

  • Partners understand how the programme works.
  • Everyone knows where to find their referral links.
  • Partners receive useful reminders and information.
  • Questions are answered or directed appropriately.
  • Successful referrals and payments are recognised.
  • The programme is represented professionally and transparently.

We want partners to have a smooth experience without unnecessary pressure or noise.

Geeslane provides the service. Partners provide the connections. We reward successful referrals.
